# Recovery policy

Verified private recovery copies preserve original refs, catalogue data and excluded historical artifacts. Locations, archive hashes, access details and the exact administrative recovery procedure belong in the owner's private migration plan, not this public tree.

Do not restore contaminated old refs to the public repository after sensitive-data removal. A normal revert is also unsafe if it reintroduces credentials, account captures or unapproved assets. Select a verified clean recovery point and review its reachability and content before any ref update. Old history remains a private forensic/recovery source.
